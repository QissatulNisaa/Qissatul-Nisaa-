Women in the Quran

Number of Women Mentioned in the Qur'an

Several thematic tafsīr studies indicate that the number of female figures whose stories are narrated in the Qur'an is relatively limited, generally estimated to range 7-12 principal figures, depending on the criteria of classification employed (Jannah & Erwanto, 2023; Pratama, 2025).

Jannah, S., & Erwanto, D. (2023). Wanita Inspiratif dalam Kajian Tafsir Al-Qur'an. REVELATIA Jurnal Ilmu al-Quran dan Tafsir. https://doi.org/10.19105/revelatia.v4i1.7254.

Pratama, Y. (2025). Spiritual Transformation and Moral Exemplars: An Analysis of Female Figures in Qur'anic Narratives. AN NUR: Jurnal Studi Islam. https://doi.org/10.37252/annur.v17i1.1701.

Women Named in the Quran

One Name, Eternal Grace. Maryam holds a unique place in the Quran as the only woman explicitly named, with Surah Maryam dedicated to her exemplary life. She represents the pinnacle of devotion, teaching us that while names may be hidden, the value of one's faith and virtue is never lost in the sight of Allah.

Honored Roles

The Quran presents women in diverse roles: prophets' mothers, queens, believers, and righteous servants of Allah. Each story carries profound lessons about faith, patience, wisdom, and courage.
